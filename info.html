AI news, straight from the source:O

CatNews checks the AI labs themselves — their GitHub releases, Hugging Face uploads, blogs and sitemaps — and writes up anything new within minutes. Hacker News discussion is shown alongside so you can see what people think.

How it works

01WatchEvery minute, our newsroom checks each lab's releases, model uploads, blog feed and sitemap.
02WriteAnything new becomes a story with our own headline and a short summary from the source.
03Time itWe log when it was published and when we caught it. See the numbers on /our-speed.
